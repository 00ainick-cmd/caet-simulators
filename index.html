STRAIGHT TALK

You know the NCATT AET. Well, here's the deal - the industry built something better. CAET (Certified Aircraft Electronics Technician) isn't just another cert with outdated bench tech questions. This is actual workforce readiness training that takes someone who knows absolutely nothing and gets them ready to work in a real hangar.

No more studying vacuum tubes and analog TV repair. CAET covers what techs actually do - troubleshooting modern avionics, understanding data buses, working with pitot-static systems. You know, the stuff that matters when you're staring at a G1000 at 2 AM.
